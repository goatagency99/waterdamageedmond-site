Homeowner questions

Water Damage Edmond FAQ

How fast can you respond for water damage in Edmond?+

Response is prioritized because drying time matters, and we aim to get a technician on site as quickly as scheduling allows. If you can safely stop the source and shut off power to affected areas, do that first, then we can begin extraction and moisture mapping upon arrival.

What should I do first if I have water damage at home?+

Stop the water source if it is safe, then protect people and pets by avoiding wet electrical areas and slippery floors. Move small valuables and electronics out of the affected zone if you can, and leave saturated materials in place until they can be evaluated so the scope is accurate.

Do you handle both residential and commercial water damage cleanup?+

Yes, we provide water damage cleanup for homes and for many small to mid size commercial spaces. The drying approach is tailored to the materials and how the space is used, so businesses can often keep limited operations running during the dry-out.

Can you dry hardwood floors and drywall, or do they always need to be removed?+

Some hardwood and drywall can be saved, but it depends on how long they were wet, how far moisture traveled, and whether the water was clean or contaminated. We make that call using moisture readings and material condition, then document the decision in the written scope.

Will you help with insurance documentation for water damage claims?+

Yes, we can provide photo documentation, drying notes, and moisture readings that support what was found on site. That helps keep communication consistent between you, your adjuster, and any repair teams involved.

Is it safe to stay in the house during the drying process?+

Many people can stay in the home during drying, but it depends on the affected rooms, the type of water, and whether containment is needed. We will walk you through what areas to avoid and how equipment placement and airflow impact comfort and noise.
