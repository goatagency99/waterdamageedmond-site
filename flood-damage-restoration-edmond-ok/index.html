Flood damage restoration questions in Edmond, OK

How fast should flood damage restoration start after water gets in?+

Restoration should start as soon as the water source is stopped and the area is safe, ideally the same day. In Edmond's humid periods, wet carpet, pad, and drywall can hold moisture and raise indoor humidity quickly, which increases the chance of odor and microbial growth.

What is the difference between floodwater and a clean water leak for cleanup?+

Floodwater is often treated as contaminated because it can carry soil, bacteria, and chemicals, while a clean supply line leak may start as potable. The category of water affects what materials can be safely salvaged and what level of cleaning and sanitizing is required.

Can you dry carpet and pad, or do they have to be removed?+

Sometimes carpet can be saved, but pad is frequently removed if it is saturated or if the water is contaminated. The decision depends on water category, how long it sat, and whether moisture readings show the subfloor and tack strip can be dried without trapping moisture.

Do you need to remove drywall after a flood?+

Drywall removal depends on how high water wicked and whether insulation or the wall cavity is wet. If moisture is present behind the wall, removing a controlled cut of drywall often speeds drying and reduces the chance of hidden damp spots remaining.

How long does the drying part of flood restoration usually take?+

Most drying takes about 3 to 5 days, but it can be longer for dense materials, multi-room losses, or when water reached wall cavities and insulation. We verify progress with moisture checks rather than relying on a set number of days.

What should I do right now while I am waiting for a restoration crew?+

Stop the water if possible, keep people out of unsafe areas, and avoid running HVAC if it could spread humidity or contaminants. If it is safe, move small valuables and electronics off the floor, and take photos of affected rooms for documentation before items are disturbed.

Will you work with insurance for flood damage restoration in Edmond, OK?+

Yes, we can provide documentation, photos, and a clear scope that helps support an insurance claim. Coverage depends on your policy and the source of the water, so it is smart to start with a documented inspection and then coordinate next steps.
